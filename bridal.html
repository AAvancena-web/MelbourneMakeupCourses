Investment

Packages & Booking

Packages start from $1200. Enquire to receive Maggie's full bridal offerings, current availability and a quote built around your day.

What's Included

  • Personal consultation to plan the look around your dress, hair and schedule.
  • Skin prep with long-wear products chosen for a full day of photography.
  • False lashes, individual or strip, to suit your eye shape.
  • A running order agreed with you and your photographer before the morning.
  • Touch-up guidance so you can keep the look fresh through the day.

The Trial

  • Held at the studio, usually 6 to 8 weeks before the wedding.
  • Bring inspiration images, your veil or hair pieces, and a top in a similar tone to your dress.
  • Photographed in daylight so you can see the finish the way a camera will.
  • Notes and product list kept on file for the morning.

Travel & Locations

  • Maggie comes to your getting-ready address.
  • Melbourne metro included; regional and interstate quoted on enquiry.
  • Early starts accommodated where the timeline needs one.
  • Tell us the address in your enquiry and it will be confirmed with your quote.

On the Day

  • A timeline built backwards from your ceremony and photographer's arrival.
  • Bride finished last so the makeup is at its freshest for photographs.
  • A calm, unhurried room from first face to last.
  • Kit on hand for any final touch-ups before you leave.

Deposits & Payments

  • A deposit secures your date; the balance is payable ahead of the day.
  • Dates are held only once the deposit is received.
  • Bookings open up to 4 months in advance for studio services; bridal dates are taken further out.

Cancellations & Credits

  • Cancel at least 7 days before the appointment and the deposit becomes a credit, valid for 12 months.
  • Cancellations within 7 days forfeit the deposit.
  • Exceptions for documented emergencies can be considered at our discretion.

Good to know

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?
As early as you can — popular dates go first, particularly through spring and autumn. Send your date through even if the rest of the planning is still up in the air.
Do you travel to me on the morning?
Yes. Maggie comes to your getting-ready address. Tell us the location in your enquiry and it will be confirmed with your quote.
Is a trial included?
Trials are covered in Maggie's bridal offerings — the details come back with your enquiry so you can see exactly what each package includes.
Can you look after my bridal party?
Yes. Let us know in the enquiry how many people need makeup and we'll build a timeline that gets everyone ready without a rush.
What if I need to change or cancel my date?
Cancel at least 7 days before the appointment and your deposit becomes a credit valid for 12 months. Within 7 days the deposit is forfeited. Exceptions for documented emergencies can be considered at our discretion.
How do I secure my date?
Send the enquiry form below. You'll receive Maggie's bridal offerings and, once you've chosen, a deposit locks the date in.
