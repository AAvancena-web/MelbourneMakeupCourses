Learn from the best in the field—our experienced beauty training academy instructors are industry leaders who bring real-world expertise and insights directly to you. Success Story

From Student to Award-Winning Stylist

Meet Ashleigh

We're thrilled to spotlight one of our incredible graduates, Ashleigh, who has truly made her mark in the beauty industry!

Ashleigh completed her hair apprenticeship with one of our esteemed former hairstyling trainers, Elesha (@hairbyelesharae), plus both the beginners and advanced makeup courses at TBA — and gained hands-on experience at wedding expos with Maggie.

Today she's the proud owner of @loveisinthehairbyashleigh, working as a hairdresser in Hoppers Crossing, a full-time mobile stylist for weddings and events, and running her own home studio — travelling all over Victoria to deliver her amazing services.

  • 2nd — ABIA Bridal Hairstylist, VIC 2024
  • 2nd — WIA Award, Greater Melbourne 2024

We couldn't be prouder of all Ashleigh has accomplished since graduating from TBA. Her journey is a testament to the hard work and passion that drives our students to success. Keep shining, Ashleigh! ✨
